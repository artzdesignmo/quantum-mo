1Qubit

A qubit is not just 0 or 1 — it can be a blend of both at once. The arrow inside each Bloch sphere is the qubit's state: up means 0, down means 1, anywhere else means a true quantum blend.

2Superposition

Drop an H gate on a wire and the qubit spreads across 0 and 1 — the probability bars split 50/50. Measure it 1000 times and the histogram lands roughly half-half, like a perfect coin.

3Entanglement

H on the top wire plus CNOT links the two qubits into a Bell pair: neither has its own state any more (watch its Bloch arrow shrink), yet measuring one instantly decides the other. Try the Bell preset.
