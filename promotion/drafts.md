## Reddit r/SideProject

**Title:** I built a pocket quantum circuit lab — drag gates, watch entanglement happen live (single HTML file)

**Body:**
Quantum Mo is a drag-and-drop quantum circuit simulator that runs entirely in the browser — no backend, no install, one HTML file.

- 9 gates (H, X, Y, Z, S, T, Rx, Ry, CNOT) on a 2-qubit circuit
- Live state vector, measurement probabilities, and draggable Bloch spheres
- An entanglement meter (concurrence) — build a Bell pair and watch both Bloch arrows vanish
- Time scrubber: replay the circuit gate by gate
- 1000-shot measurement sampler with histogram
- Every circuit encodes into the URL, so you can share exactly what you built

It's an educational simulator of ideal qubits (real linear algebra, not a real quantum computer — the page says so up front). Vanilla JS + Canvas, zero dependencies, MIT.

Try the Bell pair preset first — that's the 10-second wow. Feedback welcome, especially on the physics!
